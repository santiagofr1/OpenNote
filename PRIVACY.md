# Vynote Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026.

Vynote is a free, open-source handwriting note-taking app (https://github.com/santiagofr1/Vynote).
This page explains what data it uses and where that data goes.

## What data it uses

- **Your notebooks** (strokes, text, images, audio and how they're organized) are stored on your device.
- **If you turn on Google Drive sync**, Vynote uses Google's `drive.file` permission: it can only view and modify the
  files **it creates itself** in your Drive (the `Vynote` folder). It can't read or touch the rest of your files.
  From your Google account it only uses your email address, to show you which account you're connected with.

## Where it goes

- Your data goes **directly from your device to your own Google Drive**. It doesn't pass through any Vynote or
  developer server: there is no such server.
- Vynote **does not collect** personal data, usage statistics, advertising or tracking of any kind.
- Vynote does not sell, share or transfer your data to third parties. Its use of information received from Google
  APIs complies with the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
  including the Limited Use requirements.
- To check for updates, the app queries GitHub's public releases page; that request doesn't send any of your data.

## How to delete your data

- From the app: "Google Drive sync" → "Disconnect" revokes access.
- In your Drive, you can delete the `Vynote` folder whenever you want.
- You can also remove access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
- Uninstalling the app deletes the data saved on the device.

## Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at https://github.com/santiagofr1/Vynote/issues.
